The problem

Big screen, small apartment.
Pick one.

A 65-inch TV costs a month of rent and owns your wall forever. Projectors used to mean a dark room, a tripod, and 20 minutes of fiddling with focus.

01

TVs are a one-way purchase

You buy it, you mount it, you keep it for eight years. Moving apartments means selling it at half price or leaving it behind.

02

Your phone screen isn't a movie night

Holding a 6-inch screen on your chest is not cinema. But "watch on the wall" still sounds like a gadget project, not a Tuesday.

03

Most projectors need a small ritual

Find the keystone menu. Turn off the lights. Move it two inches left. Repeat. Half the fun is gone before the film starts.

How it works

Set it down. It does the rest.

No menus, no remote calibration, no app to pair on first use.

Put it anywhere

Coffee table, nightstand, a stack of books. Up to 30° off-axis and it still squares the picture on its own.

It focuses itself

A time-of-flight sensor measures the wall and locks focus in under a second. Move it mid-film and it refocuses without a pause.

Cast or plug in

AirPlay, Cast and Miracast built in. Or run a stick, console or laptop off the HDMI 2.1 port without a dongle chain.
